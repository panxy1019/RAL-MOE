\paragraph{Specialist architectures and deployment configurations.}
The verified configurations comprise five structured sparse-MoE
models (Circular $H/P$ and Square $S/H/P$) and three Deep-FNN-H3
Pinball predictors bound to the formal outer final-test candidates.
The Pinball outer-candidate identification does not automatically
resolve every earlier standalone result. Circular $S$ remains
unresolved and is not represented by a substitute diagnostic model.

For sparse MoE, $D=13+2n_{\rm harm}+5(2d_u+d_p)$, where
$n_{\rm harm}$ counts configured phase harmonics. Features include
parameter terms, current coefficients and Galerkin RHS, 11 derived
norm/energy/ratio quantities, and two historical blocks containing
states, right-hand sides, and differences from the current values.
The time step is an integrator input, not a network feature.
Each encoder has three linear layers of width $h$ with LayerNorm
and SiLU; the first two include dropout 0.04. Two refinement residual
blocks follow. Velocity and pressure share this encoder, but not
their router or expert parameters.

Each group contains six routed experts and one shared expert per
channel, with separate velocity/pressure Top-2 supports. Routers
receive $[\bm z;\bm\xi]$ and use LayerNorm, a width-$h$ SiLU hidden
layer, dropout 0.04, and a linear logit head. Each expert projects
$[\bm z;\bm s^c]$ to width $h$, applies $B$ expanded residual
feed-forward blocks with GELU, and maps to the channel output.
Residual scales are learned scalars initialized to 0.1.
The explicit linear branch has no bias; the two quadratic factors
each have shape $d_c\times4\times\dim(\bm s^c)$, with
$\kappa=0.05$ and no additional output projection. The branches are
added within each expert before sparse mixture weighting. Outputs
are converted from standardized target coordinates before combination
with physical-model terms.

The Pinball Deep-FNN-H3 feature width is $D=13+5(2d_u+d_p)$.
H3 denotes three-state history, not network depth. The shared trunk
starts with input LayerNorm and contains six width-512 linear layers:
one $D\to512$ projection and five $512\to512$ layers. Each uses SiLU;
the latter five also use LayerNorm. There are no trunk residual
connections or dropout. Three independent heads share the trunk.

Deployment must be distinguished from stored architecture.
Square $S$ freezes velocity experts in its final training configuration
and bypasses the stored confidence head in baseline pressure closure.
Other modules follow the training entry point's freeze settings.
Square $H$ directly uses learned velocity and pressure outputs in
overlap evaluation, without additive Galerkin/PPE contributions.
Square $P$ fixes group index 1 and uses eight internal substeps,
with maximum integrator step 0.5. A complete physical-rollout active
parameter trace has not been established for the Square models;
total and trainable counts cannot substitute for active counts.

In the original Circular $H/P$ checkpoints, both quadratic factors
remain zero after joint zero initialization. The stored rank-four
branch therefore contributes no response. The repaired initialization
retains one random factor and zeros only the other, allowing learning
from zero initial output. Activation does not establish a robust
accuracy gain: the repaired Periodic mean did not improve, and only
two of three repaired Hopf seeds were accepted. Original and repaired
configurations remain separate experimental families.
