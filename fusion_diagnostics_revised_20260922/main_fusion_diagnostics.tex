\paragraph{Physical-field diagnostics of fusion.}
On the same Square evaluation windows, T2-C has a lower offline
pressure residual than either candidate at both interfaces. However,
Hopf has a lower H--P momentum residual and a smaller S--H kinetic-energy
error: fusion does not uniformly minimize these diagnostics.
These comparisons use reconstructed fields and approximate derivatives,
not native CFD residuals, and do not establish conservation or long-time
phase stability. Conditional fusion identities, measurement protocols,
and complete results are provided in
Appendices~\ref{app:fusion_consistency} and~\ref{app:fusion_measured}.
