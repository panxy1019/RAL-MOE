\subsection{Regime-local hybrid specialists}
\label{sec:hybrid_specialists}

Each regime-local specialist combines the projected dynamics from
Section~\ref{sec:local_roms} with learned corrections in its own reduced
coordinates. Velocity is advanced dynamically according to
\begin{equation}
  \dot{\bm a}^{(r)}
  =\mathcal F_r^G(\bm a^{(r)},\bm b^{(r)};\mu)
  +\bm\rho^u_{\theta_r}(\bm\xi^{(r)}),
  \label{eq:main_specialist}
\end{equation}
whereas pressure is reconstructed algebraically after each velocity step.
This additive formulation describes the hybrid specialists; the
centered-square overlap evaluator instead uses a data-only advancement
path for its Hopf candidate, as specified in
Appendix~\ref{app:implementation}.

\paragraph{Local features.}
Let $\bm g_n^{(r)}=\mathcal F_r^G(\bm a_n^{(r)},\bm b_n^{(r)};\mu)$.
The feature vector and its learned embedding are
\begin{equation}
  \bm\xi_n^{(r)}
  =\operatorname{Feat}_r\!\left(
    (\bm a_{n-\ell}^{(r)},\bm b_{n-\ell}^{(r)},
     \bm g_{n-\ell}^{(r)})_{\ell=0}^{2},\mu\right),
  \qquad
  \bm z_n^{(r)}=\operatorname{Enc}_r(\bm\xi_n^{(r)}).
  \label{eq:main_specialist_features}
\end{equation}
The feature map standardizes local states, projected right-hand sides,
and parameter features using statistics from the corresponding
chart-specific training set. The embedding $\operatorname{Enc}_r$ acts
on these features and is distinct from the physical-field POD encoding.

\paragraph{Hierarchical sparse corrections.}
An internal router selects an expert group
$m^\star=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{m\in\mathcal G_r}
\pi_{r,m}^{\mathrm{grp}}(\bm z,\bm\xi)$,
where $\mathcal G_r$ is the set of groups in specialist $r$;
$m^\star$ is predetermined for single-group or fixed-group instances.
Within that group, separate velocity and pressure routers select the
two highest-scoring routed experts, denoted by $\mathcal T_2^c$ for
$c\in\{u,p\}$, alongside an always-active shared expert:
\begin{equation}
  \bm\rho^c_{\theta_r}(\bm\xi)
  =\omega_0^c E_{0,m^\star}^c(\bm z,\bm s^c)
   +\sum_{e\in\mathcal T_2^c}
       \omega_e^c E_{e,m^\star}^c(\bm z,\bm s^c),
  \qquad
  \omega_e^c\geq0,\quad
  \omega_0^c+\sum_{e\in\mathcal T_2^c}\omega_e^c=1.
  \label{eq:main_inner_moe}
  \label{eq:app_channel_sparse_moe}
\end{equation}
This internal routing selects correction experts within a local chart,
not regime-local specialists. The channel-specific state inputs are
$\bm s^u=\widetilde{\bm a}^{(r)}$ and
$\bm s^p=\operatorname{col}(\widetilde{\bm a}^{(r)},
\widetilde{\bm b}^{(r)})$, where tildes denote standardized coordinates.
For output component $j$, the structured expert is
\begin{equation}
  [E_{e,m}^c(\bm z,\bm s^c)]_j
  =[N_{e,m}^c(\bm z,\bm s^c)]_j
   +[W_{e,m}^{c,\mathrm{lin}}\bm s^c]_j
   +\kappa\sum_{\ell=1}^{q_b}
     \bigl({\bm v_{e,m,j\ell}^{c,(1)}}^\top\bm s^c\bigr)
     \bigl({\bm v_{e,m,j\ell}^{c,(2)}}^\top\bm s^c\bigr).
  \label{eq:main_structured_expert}
\end{equation}
Here $N_{e,m}^c$ is a nonlinear feature-conditioned network; the
remaining terms provide explicit linear and low-rank quadratic
responses, with $q_b=4$ and $\kappa=0.05$ in the specified
parameterization. In the audited Circular H/P instances, the shared
weight is $4/7$ and the remaining $3/7$ is distributed proportionally
to the selected routed-expert scores. This split is not assumed for
all benchmark configurations.

The quadratic branch was inactive in the original Circular checkpoints
because both multiplicative factors were initialized at zero. The
repaired configuration randomizes one factor while initializing the
other at zero, allowing learning from an initially zero output.
Neither the original results nor the initialization repair alone
establishes an accuracy benefit from this branch; comparative results
are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:circular}.

\paragraph{Algebraic pressure reconstruction.}
After advancing velocity, the pressure coefficients are obtained from
\begin{equation}
  \bm b_{n+1}^{(r)}
  =\bm\gamma_n^{(r)}\odot
     \mathcal Q_r^{PP}(\bm a_{n+1}^{(r)};\mu)
   +\bm\rho^p_{\theta_r}(\bm\xi_n^{(r)}),
  \qquad
  \bm\gamma_n^{(r)}
  =\operatorname{sigmoid}\!\left(
     \bm\eta^p_{\theta_r}(\bm\xi_n^{(r)})\right).
  \label{eq:main_pressure_closure}
\end{equation}
The map $\mathcal Q_r^{PP}$ is derived in Appendix~\ref{app:rom},
$\bm\gamma_n^{(r)}\in(0,1)^{d_p^{(r)}}$ modulates its contribution,
and $\odot$ denotes componentwise multiplication. Thus pressure is
not integrated as an independent dynamical variable, although its
reconstructed coefficients enter subsequent velocity updates and
the local history.
