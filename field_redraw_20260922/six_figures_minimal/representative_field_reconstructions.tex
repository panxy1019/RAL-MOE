\subsection{Representative field reconstructions}
\label{app:representative_fields_crosscase}

The following snapshots complement the aggregate routing and fusion
results with spatially resolved comparisons. For each case, we use the
terminal prediction of the first available valid window at the lowest
held-out Reynolds number in the corresponding evaluation set, rather
than selecting the snapshot with the largest improvement. All reference
fields are reconstructed from reference POD coefficients; the comparisons
therefore exclude the original CFD projection remainder.

\paragraph{Pressure reconstruction at the H--P interface.}
Figure~\ref{fig:hp_pressure_fields_supp} compares E2 Top-1 and T2-C on
the same frozen candidate trajectories. In both displayed windows,
E2 selects the Hopf candidate, whereas T2-C combines the Hopf and
Periodic reconstructions using a window-fixed weight. The full-domain,
quadrature-weighted relative pressure error at the displayed terminal
step decreases from $9.9697\%$ to $0.5382\%$ for Square and from
$0.3444\%$ to $0.0924\%$ for Pinball. These are individual-snapshot
comparisons with E2, not full-window averages or evidence that fusion
outperforms every candidate: for example, the Pinball Periodic candidate
has a lower joint velocity--pressure error than T2-C at this snapshot
($0.1075\%$ versus $0.1132\%$).

\paragraph{Velocity reconstructions across geometries.}
Figure~\ref{fig:three_case_velocity_fields_supp} provides a complementary
overview of velocity reconstruction in all three geometries. The
predictions reproduce the principal low-speed wakes, while the
vector-error maps reveal spatial discrepancies that are less apparent
from speed alone. This is a cross-case visualization of separately
trained configurations, not a matched architecture comparison or a
three-case fusion ablation: Circular uses a local specialist without
T2-C, whereas Square and Pinball use the H--P fusion configurations
shown above. Individual terminal snapshots do not establish long-time
stability or average test-set accuracy.
