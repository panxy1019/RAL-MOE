\paragraph{Physical-field diagnostics of fusion.}
To assess fusion beyond prediction error, we compare the Square
candidates and T2-C using offline residuals and kinetic-energy error.
T2-C has a lower pressure residual at both interfaces, but Hopf has
a lower H--P momentum residual and a smaller S--H energy error
(Table~\ref{tab:fusion_residual_measured}).
Figure~\ref{fig:fusion_energy_defects_supp} isolates the additional
effect of blending: the mean energy deficit relative to weighted
candidate energies is small, whereas nonlinear residual defects remain
nonzero and vary over the rollout.
These reconstructed-field diagnostics do not imply native CFD
conservation or uniformly improved physical accuracy.
Definitions and protocols are given in
Appendices~\ref{app:fusion_consistency} and~\ref{app:fusion_measured}.
