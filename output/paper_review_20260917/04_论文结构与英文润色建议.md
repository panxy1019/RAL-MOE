# 论文结构与英文润色建议

以下英文为建议稿，不是已经写入论文的版本。涉及实现细节处必须先核验；不得将待做实验写成已有结果。

## 1. 建议保留的主线

用“局部表示和动力学 + 独立轨迹的输出组装”贯穿全文。避免把创新只概括成“将 MoE 用于 ROM”，也避免声称局部坐标系在数学上无法对齐；更准确的是：本文不要求建立这种对齐映射。

标题可考虑：

> RAL-MoE-ROM: Hierarchical Mixture of Experts for Regime-Adaptive Reduced-Order Modeling

将结尾 Model 改为 Modeling 是表达建议，不要求变更现有缩写释义。全篇统一一种正式名称。

## 2. 摘要建议稿

> Reduced-order models can lose predictive accuracy when parameter variations induce qualitatively different dynamical regimes. We introduce RAL-MoE-ROM, a hierarchical framework that separates regime-local dynamics from the assembly of predictions across independently constructed reduced coordinate systems. Each local specialist augments a projected Galerkin backbone with a structured sparse mixture-of-experts closure combining nonlinear, linear, and low-rank quadratic components, and is trained through closed-loop multi-step rollouts. For a fixed-parameter prediction window, a parameter-only router provides preferences over specialists, while a validation-calibrated applicability rule restricts the candidate set. When two adjacent specialists are applicable, a gate conditioned on the initial physical history assigns a convex weight that remains fixed throughout the window. The specialists evolve independently, and only their reconstructed velocity and gauge-corrected pressure fields are blended; the fused output is not fed back into either trajectory. Experiments on three two-dimensional incompressible-flow benchmarks spanning steady, Hopf-onset, and periodic regimes demonstrate accurate autonomous predictions over the evaluated horizons and improved prediction accuracy in the tested overlap regions.

采用前核对“validation-calibrated applicability rule”是否准确描述实际过程；若仅手工区间，不要用 calibrated 掩盖。当前稿不添加速度、严格守恒、全局稳定性或完整多种子结论。

## 4. 可参考的英文段落

### 参数适应的范围（W01/W02）

> We consider autonomous prediction windows with a fixed physical parameter. The local charts are constructed before deployment, and regime adaptation refers to conditional specialist selection and output fusion. The present formulation does not perform online chart discovery or time-dependent switching between specialists within a prediction window.

这不否定某个 specialist 在固定参数下预测增长、饱和等瞬态的能力；不要把“没有切换”误写成“不能有瞬态”。

### 不同 chart 的区别

> Independently constructed charts generally have different means, bases, and scaling statistics. Their coefficient vectors are therefore not directly comparable component by component without an additional alignment map. Our assembly rule avoids requiring such a map by combining decoded physical fields.

### 融合的物理边界（M05）

> With a common discretization and compatible boundary conditions, a spatially uniform convex combination preserves linear constraints satisfied by both candidates. It does not, in general, preserve the nonlinear momentum or Pressure–Poisson equations. We therefore interpret the fused field as an output prediction rather than a state that is recursively advanced by either specialist.

如未验证候选离散无散性，保持上述条件式表达，不改成“our predictions strictly conserve mass”。

### 训练目标（M08）

> The coefficient loss uses training-set standard deviations to balance modal scales. This weighting differs from the unweighted coefficient norm associated with physical-space reconstruction error under an orthonormal POD basis. We evaluate predictive accuracy using reconstructed physical fields and do not interpret the normalized training loss as an energy-conservation constraint.

### Hopf 路由（E07）

> In the evaluated Hopf windows, the selected routed-expert support is fixed, and the velocity routed mass is concentrated on one expert. The shared expert remains active. These observations limit the evidence for dynamic expert selection in this regime and motivate comparison with a compact closure, rather than establishing that conditional routing is uniformly necessary across regimes.

### 多种子与消融边界（E03/E08）

> The reported seed variability is conditional on fixed specialists and a fixed outer router; it measures gate-training variability rather than end-to-end training uncertainty.

> The current ablations compare the available trained architectures and are not capacity-matched controls. Their results indicate performance differences under these configurations but do not fully isolate architecture from model capacity and optimization budget.

论文已基本说明这两点；重点是维持表注和结论一致，而非重复增加防御性文字。

## 5. 结果描述规则

- 表 3 中方柱 T2-C 相对 best fixed 的降低约为 30.9%（S-H）与 29.9%（H-P）；相对 E2 Top-1 则为 30.9% 与 54.2%。写清分母，不能混用。
- 这里是误差的相对降低，不是降低 30.9 个百分点。
- best fixed 是事后按评估集选出的参照，不是部署策略。
- 近全窗口 oracle 不意味着达到终点指标的理论最优；只描述实际对应目标。
- “stable”如果仅指未触发发散标准，应给经验标准；避免写成证明了稳定性。
- “efficient”在未获得计时前改成具体的 sparse activation 描述。
- 专家权重变化不自动等于发现了可解释的物理机制。
